Unread index walk alert: the slowest walk per minute, 10-minute average

The hs26 outage (fds exhausted every 2–3 days) came from one account's 5,497-page unread index: every mark-as-read walked the whole chain for ~21 s, past the 20 s relay timeout, so the client resent and hs26 walked 98% of the time. #4318 bounds a walk to the pages holding the newest 10,000 unread. This rule averages the slowest walk per minute (histogram @max) over 10 minutes with zag's function-key evaluator: warn above 5 s, critical above 10 s. The average keeps a loop whose slow walks skip minutes reading high in every check (the raw per-minute max alternates and never gets 5 bad checks in a row) and keeps single slow walks quiet. A fleet-wide failure ratio was rejected: one looping user is ~0.2% of ~1,400 walks a minute. Backtest: the 11-04 loop, and the same loop thinned to every other minute, both stay critical; nothing else would notify. Provisional: under an hour of fleet-wide data on the fix — re-check after a week.
